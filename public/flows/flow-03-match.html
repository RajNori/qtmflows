Edge cases
⏱ No quotes after 60s Client banner offers: widen radius (15km), bump price (+$20), or schedule for tomorrow. Job stays open.
↩ Client cancels mid-quote Helpers see "Job withdrawn" toast. Quotes voided. No fee. Client gets full refund.
👻 Helper accepts then ghosts No location ping in 10 min · job auto-released, client re-prompted, helper rating −0.2.
💱 Price negotiation Helper quotes $160 instead of $140. Client sees both prices side-by-side; can accept cheaper or pay extra.
